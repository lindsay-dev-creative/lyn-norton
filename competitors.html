Who else the builders are already looking at

Six national firms working at the tier Kirby Lane is aiming for, then the ten Upstate firms Lyn is realistically compared against. Every name and URL was verified against the firm's own site or a first-party directory listing.

National benchmarks

Six firms working nationally at the tier Kirby Lane is aiming for. None of them publish a minimum, so treat the spend framing as inference from scope rather than a stated number. Each one answers a question Lyn's rebrand has to answer.

studio-mcgee.com

What the scan actually tells you

  • The real threat is not aesthetic, it is structural. Two of the ten are owned by builders. Those firms never appear on the list of three that a project coordinator hands a homeowner, because they are the default. Kirby Lane's builder-relationship goal has to account for that.
  • Founder names dominate. Five of ten are a person's name. Kirby Lane sitting outside that pattern is a genuine differentiator, and it supports the Properties and Collective extension without a second rebrand.
  • The stated vocabulary is nearly identical across the field. Timeless, curated, thoughtful, full-service, warm. Almost every site uses at least three of those. Lyn's construction fluency is the only claim in this set that no one else can make.
  • The type story is one note. Across the five sampled, every logo is a serif in all caps over a letterspaced sans, and every body face is a neutral sans. Nobody is doing anything with type. Fowler runs three unrelated voices in one screen, a didone wordmark, a geometric sans nav, and an italic serif button. Swander's lockup stacks a serif monogram, a sans wordmark, and a handwritten script. Both read as accumulated rather than designed.
  • Color confirms the whitespace. Sampled backgrounds are #FFFFFF, #F9F8F7, #F7F5F1, and #F9F7F5. Four firms, four versions of the same off white. Accents are a teal, a deep olive, a sage, and a chartreuse. Lyn's dark and moody direction is genuinely unoccupied in this set, which is the strongest evidence yet for the deep, nature derived palette.
  • One firm is closer to Lyn's aesthetic than expected. In Site pairs near black at #0C0C0C with a dark, dramatic hero. Worth a look before finalizing direction, since it is the only site here that is not committed to light and airy.
  • Site quality is uneven and beatable. Wix, PhotoBiz, Shopify, and a WordPress site running a wedding stationery sideline. A well-built site is a real advantage here, which strengthens the case for the full rebuild after the identity lands.
  • Correction to carry forward: Fowler Designs is Fowler Interiors. It appears wrong in the brief and would appear wrong in the deck.
